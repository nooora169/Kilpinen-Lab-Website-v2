# Publication audit — 10 September 2026

Selection source: https://www.helsinki.fi/en/researchgroups/cellular-genetics-of-disease/publications

The website now contains the same 13 selected works as this official lab page. This is a selected bibliography, not every paper coauthored by a group member. The official page uses ds-link elements; their ds-text and ds-href attributes were read directly to retrieve titles and destinations omitted by text-only extraction.

## Changes

- Corrected the Puigdevall 2023 title to Effects of somatic mutations on cellular differentiation in iPSC models of neurodevelopment.
- Retained Budinger 2026's published title “profile”, confirmed at https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-67779-1. Helsinki still labels its link “atlas”.
- Retained Sundaresh 2025's title and DOI, both confirmed by Helsinki's link attributes. The old lab site's 2023 preprint has a different title.
- Retained Saukkonen 2022's published title without the preprint's PAC prefix, matching the Genome Research publisher search record. Helsinki uses the PAC prefix but links to the same article.
- Added the five selected older papers from 2018, 2017, 2015, 2014 and 2013, with the exact DOI destinations recorded by Helsinki. Normalized malformed doi: links to https://doi.org/.
- Removed Roth, Neaverson, Sokka, Cuomo and McCarthy entries from the selected list because they are not in the current official selection. This does NOT establish that all those papers are unrelated to the lab. Roth and Neaverson include Kilpinen as a coauthor; Neaverson's published year was also wrong in the old site.
- Removed the unverified CRISPR-topic/Sokka association and the off-selection Roth topic reference. No replacement project associations were invented.
- Publication DOI links and GitHub resource links remain direct. People and lab information links now use the official Helsinki site.

## Maintenance

Every selected publication records sourceUrl. The fetch-publications script writes candidates to publications.import.json; it must never automatically overwrite the curated live list. Check title, DOI, author list, year, publication status and lab selection before adding a candidate. A topic-to-paper association requires separate relevance confirmation.

Publisher pages were not all independently accessible; Helsinki is the authoritative selection and metadata source for those records. The current first three papers are explicitly present on its selected list.
