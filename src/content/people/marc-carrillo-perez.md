Rare genetic disorders have been a common topic of discussion since early years of my life, as I got to know from first-hand how tough the journey of acceptance and adaptation to an unexpected condition can be for the patients and their family. This life situation made me determined to address their questions and concerns through research, which at the same time stemmed my passion for understanding how genetics shape the molecular mechanisms of life. To gain the expertise for such mission, I completed a Bachelor’s in Biomedical Sciences at the University of Barcelona (2019–2023), and a Master’s in Genetics and Molecular Biosciences at the University of Helsinki (2023–2025). In May 2024, I joined the Kilpinen lab to explore the possibilities that combining hiPSCs, CRISPR genome editing and high-throughput assays offer to model neurodevelopmental disorders and understand their underlying molecular alterations. My doctoral research has a particular focus on disease mechanisms involving splicing alterations. When I am not at the lab, you will find me hiking, enjoying football or doing some weightlifting!
